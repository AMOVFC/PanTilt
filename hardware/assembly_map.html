Open one bag, place every part from it, tick it off, move to the next. SMD parts first (bottom, then top), then the through-hole parts from shortest to tallest so the board sits flat on the bench while you solder.

pantilt-controller.kicad_pcb · 52.3 × 93.2 mm · 40 SMD + 35 through-hole
Check D1 and D2 before soldering. The schematic puts the cathode on pad 2 (the +24 V / 5 V pad), but the D_SMB footprint's silkscreen bar sits on pad 1 (GND). Put the diode's band on the rail pad, as drawn here, not on the silk bar. Backwards, the TVS shorts the rail and blows the fuse at power-up.
